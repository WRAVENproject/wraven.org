Privacy policy

What this site collects, which other companies see your visit, and how to ask us to delete what we hold.

Last updated November 22, 2026.

Hosting

wraven.org is a set of static files. GitHub Pages hosts the files, and Cloudflare sits in front of GitHub Pages.

Both companies process each request that your browser sends. A request includes your IP address, your browser's user agent, the time, and the page. Each company handles this data under its own policy:

  • GitHub General Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Analytics and cookies

WRAVEN runs no analytics and no ad scripts. We set no cookies of our own.

Cloudflare features

Network Error Logging is on for this site. It asks your browser to report failed requests, and your browser can then send error reports to Cloudflare.

In your browser

The fonts on this site come from wraven.org itself. The main pages do not use Google Fonts.

Some pages install a service worker. It keeps copies of pages and files on your device, so that they open without a connection. To remove the copies, clear the site data for wraven.org in your browser settings.

The next-meeting line at the top of the page is computed in your browser. Nothing is sent to us.

Contact form

The form on Work with us collects your name, your email address, your organization if you give one, the topic that you choose, and your message.

The page runs Cloudflare Turnstile, an anti-bot check, as soon as it opens, even if you send nothing. Cloudflare runs the check and sees your IP address. When you send the form, our Worker sends the challenge token and your IP address to Cloudflare to confirm the check.

A Cloudflare Worker then saves the message in a Cloudflare D1 database. WRAVEN officers read the messages there. The Worker also posts a notice in a private officer channel on Discord. The notice names only the topic that you chose and a message number.

We delete form messages after 12 months.

Email

Our mail provider keeps the email that you send to contact@wraven.org. For sensitive mail, encrypt it with our PGP key.

Other services

This site links to services that WRAVEN does not run. Each one follows its own privacy policy:

  • our blog, which beehiiv hosts
  • Discord
  • donations through HCB
  • GitHub
  • ExperienceWMU
