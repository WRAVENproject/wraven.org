Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 22, 2026

Overview

WRAVEN (Western Research Advisory for Vulnerabilities, Exploits, & Networks) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website and use our services.

Information We Collect

Automatically Collected

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information:

  • IP Address: For security monitoring and analytics
  • Browser Type & Version: To ensure compatibility
  • Device Information: Basic device and screen data
  • Access Logs: Timestamps and pages visited
  • Referrer Information: Where you came from (when available)

Voluntarily Provided

We only collect information you choose to provide:

  • Research Submissions: Academic or professional inquiries
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Email address for updates

How We Use Information

Primary Uses

  • Security: Monitor for threats and protect infrastructure
  • Communication: Respond to inquiries and provide updates
  • Analytics: Understand usage patterns to improve services
  • Research: Aggregate anonymized data for cybersecurity research

We Do Not

  • Sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties
  • Use tracking cookies for advertising
  • Store sensitive personal information unnecessarily
  • Profile users for commercial purposes

Data Protection & Security

Security Measures

As a cybersecurity organization, we implement industry-standard practices:

  • Encryption: All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS
  • Access Controls: Strict authentication and authorization
  • Incident Response: Established procedures for security events

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary:

  • Contact Information: Until you request removal
  • Access Logs: 90 days for security purposes
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized after 1 year

Third-Party Services

External Links

Our website links to external services (GitHub, Discord, etc.). These services maintain their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their practices.

Hosted Services

Our website may use minimal third-party services:

  • Fonts: Google Fonts (cached locally when possible)
  • Analytics: Self-hosted analytics (no external tracking)
  • CDN: Content delivery networks for performance

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request information about what data we hold
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability: Request data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from communications at any time

We aim to honor the rights described above for all visitors regardless of region.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal cookies and local storage:

  • Session Cookies: Basic site functionality
  • Preferences: Theme and language settings
  • Security: CSRF protection

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

Academic & Research Use

As a university-affiliated organization, some activities may involve:

  • Research Publications: Anonymized data in academic papers
  • Student Projects: Data used for educational purposes
  • Cybersecurity Research: Threat intelligence and security analysis

All research follows ethical guidelines and institutional review processes.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. For significant changes, we may provide additional notice through our website or email.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or our data practices:

  • Privacy: privacy@wraven.org
  • General: contact@wraven.org
  • Address: WRAVEN, Western Michigan University, Kalamazoo, MI 49008

We respond to privacy inquiries within 30 days.
