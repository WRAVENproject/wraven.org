How WRAVEN can help

WRAVEN staff are happy to assist businesses, nonprofits, schools, and other organizations. Tell us what you need, and we will see how we can help.

Use the contact form Our services

What we offer

On-premises security review
We come to your site, look at how your network, devices, and accounts are set up, and tell you what to fix first.
Training and talks
Sessions for your employees on the attacks that reach small organizations, such as phishing and callback scams. The Phone Is the Payload, our report on callback extortion against law firms, shows the kind of scam we teach people to spot.
Attack surface audits
We map what your organization exposes to the internet and report what an attacker would see.
CTF events
We run capture-the-flag competitions for schools, clubs, and company teams.
Something else
If your need is not on this list, tell us about it.

Before any review or audit, we agree with you in writing on what we will look at. What we find goes to you, privately. Every member signs our ethics agreement.

If you are under attack now

Call your IT provider first. You can also report it to the FBI at ic3.gov or to CISA at cisa.gov/report. WRAVEN does not offer emergency incident response.

Sponsors

Money pays for workshop materials, competition fees, and travel. These gifts also help:

  • Hardware. Routers, switches, laptops, servers, and radios, new or retired.
  • Software licenses. Seats for commercial security tools that members use in workshops and research.
  • Certification exam vouchers. For members who finish our certification study sessions.
  • Conference tickets. Student passes to security conferences, and help with travel.
  • Training platform seats. Access to hands-on labs and CTF practice platforms.
  • Cloud credits. For practice labs and member projects.
  • Your time. A talk at a meeting, a workshop, or mentoring for members.

To offer one of these, write to contact@wraven.org. To give money, donate through HCB. Donations are tax-deductible, and our supporters page explains how.

Recruiters

If your company hires for security or IT roles, use the contact form. We will share the opening with members and encourage them to apply.

Schools and faculty

WRAVEN members can visit your high school to give a talk, a presentation, or a hands-on workshop. Topics include account security, phishing, how attacks work, and what a career in security looks like.

We also run capture-the-flag (CTF) events. Our own competition, WRAVEN CTF 2026, had a high school bracket. High schools and school clubs can bring a team, or ask us to run a CTF for them.

Faculty can write to us about a class or a project. Our faculty advisor is Kevin Mu, Information Systems.
