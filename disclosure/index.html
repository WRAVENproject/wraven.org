Vulnerability disclosure policy

Version 1.2. In effect since July 22, 2026.

Overview

If you find a vulnerability in wraven.org or another WRAVEN system, email the details to contact@wraven.org. Encrypt sensitive reports with our PGP key.

Machine-readable version: /.well-known/security.txt

Scope

This policy applies to public-facing WRAVEN infrastructure, including:

  • wraven.org and its subdomains
  • Services listed or linked on our official website
  • Infrastructure owned or operated by WRAVEN, or clearly marked as such, unless otherwise stated

Out of scope

  • Student personal systems
  • WMU owned or operated infrastructure in any capacity
  • Third-party services embedded via iframe or external widgets

Rules of engagement

  • Do not intentionally access, modify, or delete data that is not yours.
  • Do not use automated scanners that cause excessive traffic.
  • Do not run denial-of-service attacks.
  • Social engineering (e.g. phishing) against WRAVEN members or WMU is out of bounds.
  • Automated, low-effort, or AI-generated reports and emails will be ignored.

Safe harbor

If you follow this policy, we will not pursue legal action against you. We are students. We appreciate your help.

Response timeline

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days and provide regular updates until the issue is resolved.
