Code of Conduct

Scope

This code is in effect whenever you are representing WRAVEN, participating in a WRAVEN activity, or present in a WRAVEN space. Meetings, competitions, online channels, public events, and any work done under the WRAVEN name are all covered.

Standards

  • Be direct, be respectful, and assume the person across from you is trying their best.
  • If you know something, share it. If you do not, say so. Nobody here is expected to have all the answers.
  • Give credit where it is earned. Represent your own work and the work of others accurately.
  • Leave every tool, system, and workspace in better shape than you found it.
  • All members sign and are held to the WRAVEN White Hat & Ethics Agreement, which governs how security tools and knowledge are used.
  • All members are expected to follow the WMU Student Code of Conduct and all applicable law.

Prohibited Conduct

  • Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
  • Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention
  • Publishing, threatening to publish, or seeking out another person's private information
  • Deliberately and repeatedly disrupting meetings, workshops, or competitions
  • Retaliating against anyone who makes a report under this policy

Reporting

If something is wrong, say something. Report to any officer, or contact the Compliance Lead directly at compliance@wraven.org. If the concern involves an officer, go to any other officer.

Reports are acknowledged within 48 hours. All reports are handled discreetly.

Enforcement actions are defined in the WRAVEN Constitution. When warranted, matters are referred to WMU under the Student Code of Conduct or escalated to WMU Public Safety.

Report a Concern

  • Compliance Lead: compliance@wraven.org
  • Related: White Hat & Ethics Agreement
