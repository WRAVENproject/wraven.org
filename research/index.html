Tools and data

WRAVEN on GitHub

Watchtower

A public threat-intelligence dashboard that we run on OpenCTI. Anyone can open it. It collects public threat feeds such as abuse.ch ThreatFox and URLhaus, the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, MITRE ATT&CK, and the NIST National Vulnerability Database.

On November 21, 2026, it showed more than 500,000 indicators of compromise.

slantwise

Estimates where an audio recording was made. It matches the Doppler curves of propeller planes in the recording against public ADS-B flight data.

Open source under AGPL-3.0. Released in November 2026, and still in beta.

field-ops-docs

Cheat sheets that sync to members' Kali machines. “If it's in here, it's because someone actually used it.” (from its README)
