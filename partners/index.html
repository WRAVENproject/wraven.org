Partners

Partner with WRAVEN

WRAVEN is a student-led threat research and cyber operations lab at Western Michigan University. The lab produces public threat research, runs Horizon, a student-operated SOC, competes internationally in CTF events as ECX Inc., and builds open-source security tools. Partner support goes directly into that work.

Affiliations

Electronic Frontier Foundation

WRAVEN is a member of the Electronic Frontier Alliance, a grassroots network of community and student groups convened by the EFF around shared principles of free expression, privacy, creativity, and access to knowledge. WRAVEN was the first EFA member organization in Michigan. Alliance membership is an affiliation rather than a partnership or sponsorship, and it does not mean the EFF endorses WRAVEN's research, findings, or statements.

Electronic Frontier Alliance →

Bugcrowd

WRAVEN is a Bugcrowd partner organization. Bugcrowd operates a crowdsourced security platform through which researchers report vulnerabilities to participating organizations. The relationship is an organizational affiliation and does not represent an endorsement by Bugcrowd of WRAVEN's research or findings.

Bugcrowd →

Hack Club

The Hack Foundation, which operates Hack Club Bank, is WRAVEN's fiscal sponsor. Fiscal sponsorship means WRAVEN receives and holds funds under The Hack Foundation's 501(c)(3) status, which is what makes donations to WRAVEN tax-deductible. The Hack Foundation is not a funder of WRAVEN, does not grant WRAVEN nonprofit status of its own, and does not endorse WRAVEN's research or findings.

Hack Club Bank →

Funding

WSAAC

The Western Student Association Allocations Committee is the funding body of WMU's student government. It reviews requests from registered student organizations and allocates direct funding for their activity on campus. WRAVEN has received WSAAC funding as a registered student organization at Western Michigan University.

WSA Allocations →

CEAS Dean's Fund

The WMU College of Engineering and Applied Sciences RSO Excellence Fund provides competitive grant funding to registered student organizations within the college. Awards are made by application and assessed against the fund's criteria. WRAVEN has received funding from the fund.

What Funding Supports

  • Equipment: Lab hardware for research and training
  • CTF Travel & Registration: Getting the team to competitions
  • Horizon SOC Infrastructure: Servers and sensors for the student-run SOC
  • Workshop Materials: Hands-on labs for new members

Tax Status

WRAVEN is fiscally sponsored by The Hack Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 81-2908499). Donations are tax-deductible.
