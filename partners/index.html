Supporters

These organizations fund WRAVEN, hold its money, work with us, or count WRAVEN as a member, and we appreciate their support.

Affiliations

Electronic Frontier Alliance

The Electronic Frontier Alliance is a network of community and student groups that the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF) brings together. WRAVEN joined in 2025 and was the first EFA member in Michigan.

Bugcrowd

WRAVEN is a Bugcrowd partner organization. Bugcrowd runs a platform where researchers report vulnerabilities to the organizations that take part. It sends speakers to WRAVEN, gives members merch, and sponsors some of our events.

At one of our Bugcrowd events, Ads Dawson (0xmoose) joined our members by video.

Partners

Computer Club at WMU

The Computer Club is our closest partner. It is a WMU hackerspace, founded in 1976, with servers that its members run. WRAVEN meets in the Computer Club room, 2225 Kohrman Hall. The two clubs play capture-the-flag events together as ECX Inc.

WMU Cybersecurity Studies Center

The center connects the cybersecurity programs in the College of Engineering and Applied Sciences and the Haworth College of Business. It co-presents the WMU Cybersecurity Summit, and acts as the hub for cybersecurity outreach and education.

Red Bull

WRAVEN partnered with Red Bull in 2026. Members have access to a lot of free Red Bull.

Fiscal sponsor

The Hack Foundation

The Hack Foundation, the nonprofit behind Hack Club, is our fiscal sponsor. It is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 81-2908499.

Fiscal sponsorship means that WRAVEN receives and holds its money under The Hack Foundation's 501(c)(3) status. That's why donations to WRAVEN are tax-deductible!

Funding

Western Student Association Allocations Committee

WSAAC funds registered student organizations at Western Michigan University. WRAVEN is one of them. That money paid for our startup equipment, such as laptops, networking gear, and radios.

WMU CEAS RSO Excellence Fund

The RSO Excellence Fund of WMU's College of Engineering and Applied Sciences gives grants to registered student organizations that apply. Its grant to WRAVEN paid for a forensics and research workstation that we use in workshops.

What the money pays for

Money from supporters pays for workshop materials, competition fees, and travel. Hardware, software licenses, and other gifts also help. See the full list.
