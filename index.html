About WRAVEN

Western Research Advisory for Vulnerabilities, Exploits, & Networks

WMU's cybersecurity research & operations team. We analyze emerging threats, compete in international CTF events, conduct vulnerability research, and build open-source security tools.

Founded in partnership with WMU's Computer Club. Fiscally sponsored by Hack Club (The Hack Foundation), a 501(c)(3).

Get Involved

No experience required. Seriously.

Most of our members joined with zero security background. If you're a WMU student who's curious about how attacks work and how to stop them, you belong here.

Weekly meetings on campus. Times and rooms are posted on Discord and ExperienceWMU each semester. Members go on to co-ops, internships, and security careers.
